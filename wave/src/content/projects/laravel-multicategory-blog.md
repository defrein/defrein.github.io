## The idea

This blog focuses on helping readers find articles and take part in the discussion. A post can belong to several categories, while interactive listing controls support browsing and discovery.

## The implementation

- Featured and latest published articles on the public homepage.
- Many-to-many categories, search, sorting, and paginated article lists.
- Article detail pages with excerpts, reading-time helpers, comments, and likes.
- Jetstream authentication and Filament resources for managing content.
- Livewire components for the listing, search box, comments, and like button.
