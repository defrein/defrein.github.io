## The idea

A community application needs to connect people and their ideas. This project brings publishing, conversation, and follow relationships together in a familiar social feed.

## The implementation

- User authentication, editable profiles, and biographies.
- Posts with comments and like/unlike interactions.
- Follow/unfollow relationships and a dedicated feed of followed accounts.
- Search, pagination, language selection, and administrative lists for users, posts, and comments.

Screenshots were captured from the repository application running locally with fictional accounts, posts, comments, and follow relationships. No production community data is shown.
