## The idea

School administration involves linked records across students, teachers, classes, and payments. This academic project implements those workflows directly in PHP with a MySQL database.

## The implementation

- Student, teacher, and administrator account management.
- Class schedules connecting teachers, subjects, and classes.
- Tuition payment records and printable views for school records.
- PHP sessions, server-rendered forms, and mysqli database queries without a PHP application framework.

## The preview

The screenshots come from a local installation with a fresh demonstration database. Student names, teacher names, account details, and payment records were created for the capture; original school records are not published.
